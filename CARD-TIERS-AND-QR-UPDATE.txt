HASDEO BANK — CARD TIERS, QR PROFILE & CUSTOMER ACCESS LABEL UPDATE

This update is for the educational/demo project only. It does not connect to a real bank, card network, payment processor, or identity system.

CUSTOMER ACCESS CONTROL
- Owner Panel > Website Settings > Customer Access Control is written in English.
- Signup and Customer Login remain independent controls, each with its own Save button.

FIVE DEMO CARD TIERS
- Classic: navy and silver
- Silver: metallic silver
- Gold: dark gold
- Platinum: graphite and platinum
- Royal: royal purple and gold
- New signup form asks customers to choose a card tier.
- The selected tier is stored with the customer record and applied to the dashboard card theme.
- Customer dashboard and Card Management workspace include an Upgrade Card option.
- Card style changes are saved to the database and recorded in Activity Logs.

QR PROFILE
- Each customer gets a random 192-bit QR token.
- The QR opens /card.html?token=... and fetches the current public demo profile.
- The public page shows customer display name, demo customer ID, card tier, last four digits only, and account status.
- It does NOT expose full card number, transfer code, password, email, balance, or transaction history.
- Older customer records receive a QR token the next time they open their account dashboard.

DATABASE
- Hibernate ddl-auto=update adds nullable card_tier and qr_token columns to demo_customers.
- Existing customer rows remain; old card tiers default to Classic when their account is opened.
- Do not delete or reset the existing Neon database.

VALIDATION
- JavaScript syntax check passed with Node.
- HTML duplicate-ID checks passed for index.html and card.html.
- pom.xml parsed as valid XML.
